The problem: the math is easy, the inputs are not

🕒
Reports arrive at different times

A newer report may still be waiting to be checked

❓
Some shelters have no confirmed report

A new shelter can be invisible to the plan

📦
Stock moves while it is counted

Was the 08:45 truck already in the 10:00 count?

🍼
Special diets can’t be swapped

Formula, allergy-free, halal — and “unknown” is not zero

A spreadsheet can use an unchecked report; a chatbot may compute differently each time. Staff need one consistent picture from checked data, a list of what is missing, and a named person who decides.

Illustrations from the synthetic sample data (4 shelters, 2 warehouses). No field statistics are claimed.

What FoodBridge does differently

1
The AI never does the arithmetic

Needs, stock, Plan A and Plan B come from tested code. The AI only picks the calculation and explains it.

2
Uncertainty stays visible

Records are validated, pending or rejected. Only validated data counts; everything else becomes a warning.

3
AI reads, staff confirm

Reports and photos are read by AI with quoted evidence, checked by code, and saved only after staff approve.

4
A person finalizes the plan

Finalizing re-checks the data, needs an acknowledgement per warning, and records who decided.

Compared in the report with Sahana Eden, FEMA’s National Shelter System, WFP LESS and ChatGPT: a different focus, not a claim that they lack these features.
